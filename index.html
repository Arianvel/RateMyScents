      View the formulas

      Variety: 100 × (distinct profiles / 15) × min(1, fragrance count / 4).

      We use 15 profiles, including fruity, powdery and leather. White floral counts as Floral in the calculations, so a fragrance tagged with both counts once.

      The size factor is 50% for two fragrances, 75% for three and 100% from four. Below four, another fragrance can raise Variety even without new profile labels.

      Versatility: 100 × [0.5 × (occasions / 5) + 0.5 × (seasons / 4)].

      Complementarity: 100 × (1 − average profile overlap). For each pair, overlap is shared labels divided by all distinct labels in that pair.

      Total: Variety × 0.4 + Versatility × 0.3 + Complementarity × 0.3.

      How do we find fragrances for you?

      A short quiz lets you tell us which scents catch your attention, when you would wear them and how much you want to spend. We compare your answers with the fragrances in our catalog to find options worth exploring. You can open each suggestion to get to know it a little better.

      What shapes your suggestions?
      • Similar to my collection: More familiar scent profiles.
      • Different from my collection: Less overlap with what you own.
      • More occasions covered: More suggested uses.
      • No preference: More matches to your answers.

      What if I don't own a fragrance yet?

      You can start here, too. Choose “Find my first fragrance” on the home page and tell us what you would like to try. We will use your answers to suggest a few places to start, without asking you to build a collection first.

      Our method is a guide to your collection, not a verdict on your taste.
